How to Order

Ordering is easy — just send us a message and we'll take care of the rest. No apps, no accounts, no fuss.

1

Pick your breads

Choose what you'd like and how many of each from our menu.

2

Send a message

Text or email your order with your name and the day you'd like it.

3

We bake it fresh

We'll confirm your order and bake it to order — never in advance.

4

Pick it up warm

We'll text you the pickup address when your order is confirmed. Grab it fresh!

Good to Know

A few friendly details to make ordering smooth:

🗓️ Large orders. Big orders are baked and ready by the end of the day on Fridays or Saturdays — we'll confirm your exact day and time when you place your order.

⏰ Smaller orders. Smaller orders are usually quicker! We'll look at what you need and let you know when it'll be ready right when you order.

🥖 Standing weekly orders. Love your bread? Reserve the same loaf every week and we'll set it aside for you, no need to ask twice.

🎁 Refer a friend, get a thank-you treat. Tell a neighbor about us — when they place their first order, your next one comes with a little something on the house.

💵 Simple & safe. We keep ordering lightweight: no online payments or card details needed. We'll sort out payment at pickup.
